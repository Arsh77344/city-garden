Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

City Garden is a marriage lawn and banquet hall at S-14/27, Andhrapull, Chaukaghat, Grand Trunk Road, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh 221002. This page explains what happens to your information when you use citygarden.co.in.

The short version: there are no accounts and no logins here. The only thing we store is the enquiries people choose to send us. We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not run advertising on this site.

Information you give us

The enquiry form on our contact section asks for your name, phone number, the type of event, your preferred date, an approximate guest count and anything else you want to tell us.

When you submit that form, two things happen. The details are saved to our enquiry database so we have a record of your request and can follow it up. They are also assembled into a WhatsApp message that opens in your own WhatsApp, addressed to us — nothing is sent through WhatsApp until you press send there, and you can edit or abandon that message first. If you do send it, the message sits in our WhatsApp account under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Our enquiry database is hosted by Supabase on servers in Mumbai, India. Only City Garden can read what is stored there — the website itself is only able to add an enquiry, never to read one back.

If you call us or email city.gardenvns0542@gmail.com instead, we keep that correspondence so we can answer you and manage your booking.

The review page

Our review page at citygarden.co.in/review helps you turn a few answers into a review you can post on Google. Your rating, the topics you pick and anything you type stay in your browser while you use it. We do not receive them, and nothing is posted anywhere automatically — you copy the text and publish it yourself on Google, under your own Google account.

If you use the private feedback option instead, it opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. You can read it over and change it before sending.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies of its own.

We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, which pages they read and roughly where they are visiting from. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes technical information such as your IP address, browser, device type and the pages you view. We use it to improve the website — not to identify you personally, and not for advertising.

You can opt out across all websites using Google's browser add-on, or block cookies in your browser settings. The site works normally either way.

Other services this page touches

  • Google Fonts — typefaces load from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Google Maps — our contact section embeds a Google map, which loads when you scroll to it. Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, under Google's privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp — only when you choose to send an enquiry.
  • Supabase — stores the enquiries sent through our form, on servers in India.
  • Our hosting provider — like all web hosts, it keeps standard server logs including IP addresses, for security and reliability.

How long we keep things

Enquiries and booking correspondence are kept for as long as we are dealing with your event, and afterwards for our own records and any legal or tax obligations. Google Analytics data is retained according to the period set in our Analytics account.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to city.gardenvns0542@gmail.com or call +91 99196 96288 and we will respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Children

This website is intended for adults planning events. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change how this site handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
