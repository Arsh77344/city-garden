# Image sources

Every non-original image used on this site is recorded here: what it shows, where it
came from, and under what licence. `tools/add-stock.py` appends to this file and will
not add an image without a source URL.

Why this file exists: an image on a commercial site whose licence nobody can produce is
a liability, and the claim lands on the business rather than on whoever added it.

## The rules these entries follow

- **Licence must permit commercial use.** Pexels, Unsplash and Pixabay all do, with no
  attribution required. Credit the photographer here anyway — it costs nothing and it
  is how provenance stays traceable.
- **Never imply endorsement.** A stock photograph cannot appear in a logo or a
  trademark, and cannot be presented as a City Garden client, event or endorsement.
- **Alt text describes what the image shows**, never "at City Garden". A family that
  books on the strength of a photograph and arrives to find it was stock has a
  legitimate complaint, and it is the kind of thing people notice.
- **Prefer images without identifiable faces.** Free stock licences do not guarantee a
  model release, and a recognisable person used to advertise a business is a separate
  and larger problem. Hands, details, decor, wide shots and empty settings are safer
  and usually better for a blog header anyway.
- **Never a child's face.**
