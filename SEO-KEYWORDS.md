## What the data says

**"Banaras" is dead as a search term.** `marriage lawn in banaras` returns 0 and
`wedding venues in banaras` has no data at all, while the Varanasi spellings sit at
480. Do not spend words on it.

**"In varanasi" carries the volume.** `marriage lawn in varanasi` has data at 480;
`marriage lawn varanasi` without the "in" does not even register. Phrase headings and
copy the way people actually type.

**Four terms share the top spot at 480**, and they are four different words for the
same thing — banquet hall, marriage lawn, wedding venue, and the planner term. The site
should use all four naturally rather than picking one, because they are not competing
with each other.

**Difficulty is low across the board (18–23).** This is not a hard market to rank in.
The constraint is that there is not much search volume to begin with, so the goal is to
capture a high share of a small pool, not to chase traffic.

## Where these go

**Meta title** — the current one is already well aimed:
`City Garden | Marriage Lawn & Banquet Hall in Varanasi` (54 characters, fits Google's
display limit). It covers keywords 1 and 2. The gap is "wedding venue", which is
another 480/month term and is absent.

**Meta description** — already names Chaukaghat and the lawn. Worth working in "wedding
venue" and a price or capacity signal, since `banquet hall in varanasi with price` and
`marriage lawn price in varanasi` both have real searches and the site currently says
nothing about either.

**Section headings** — the venue, services and contact sections are the natural homes
for keywords 5–12. Headings carry more weight than body copy.

**Blog posts** — one post per search, written to answer it properly:

## What actually moves the needle

Honest ordering for a business this local. Keywords are necessary but they are not
where the wins are:

1. **Google Business Profile.** `banquet hall near me` is 5,400 searches and is decided
   almost entirely by the Business Profile and the map pack, not by this website.
   Category set to "Wedding venue", correct address, real photos, opening hours.
2. **Reviews.** The single strongest local ranking factor, and the site currently shows
   one review. Every booked family should be asked. The `/review/` page exists for this.
3. **Then** the keyword work above.

A site this well-built ranking for terms with KD 20 is achievable. A Google Business
Profile with ten reviews will bring more enquiries than any of it.
