# Off-site links — City Garden

Internal links are handled by `BLOG-PLAN.md`. This file is about **inbound** links,
which the blog cannot create for itself. Ordered by what will actually move enquiries,
not by what is easiest to count.

A note on what to avoid first, because it is the one way to make things worse: do not
buy links, do not submit to bulk "500 directories" packages, and do not accept guest-post
schemes. Google devalues those and they put a real local business at risk for no gain.
Everything below is a link a real business legitimately has.

## Tier 1 — do these first

**1. Google Business Profile.** Not a backlink, and still the single highest-value item
on this page. `banquet hall near me` is 5,400 searches a month and is decided by the map
pack, not by the website. Category "Wedding venue", exact address, real photos, hours,
and the website URL pointing at `https://www.citygarden.co.in/`. Then reviews — the
`/review/` page exists for exactly this, and every booked family should be asked.

**2. Claim the listings that probably already exist.** Directories build unclaimed
profiles from public business information, so there may well be a City Garden page on
one or more of these already, with wrong details and no link back. Search each for the
business name before creating anything new.

| Platform | What to do | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| WedMeGood | Claim or create the venue profile, add the website link | Free tier exists; paid tier buys placement |
| WeddingWire India | Create the storefront, link the site | Free tier exists |
| ShaadiSaga | Create the vendor profile | Free tier exists |
| Justdial | Claim the listing, correct the address | Free |
| Sulekha / IndiaMART | Secondary, worth having | Free |
| Facebook & Instagram | Website field on both profiles | Free |

A free listing on the wedding portals sits deep in their search results — the link
value is real but the referral traffic is not, so treat the free tier as an SEO task
and decide on paid placement separately, as advertising.

**3. Name, address, phone consistency.** Every listing above must carry the address
*character for character* as the site has it: S-14/27, Andhrapull, Chaukaghat, Grand
Trunk Road, Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh 221002, and `+91 99196 96288`. Inconsistent
listings split the signal and are the most common local-SEO mistake.

## Tier 2 — the links that are actually earned

**4. Vendor cross-links.** The photographers, caterers, decorators and planners who
already work at City Garden have websites and portfolios. A link exchange here is
natural, relevant and free: they publish a gallery from a wedding held here and credit
the venue; the venue's journal links the vendor in a post about their trade. Start with
whoever shot the photographs already on the site.

**5. Real weddings features.** Wedding portals and blogs publish submitted real-wedding
stories and credit every vendor with a link. One good shoot submitted to WedMeGood's or
ShaadiSaga's real-weddings section is worth more than twenty directory entries. Needs
the couple's permission and the photographer's files.

**6. Local press and city listings.** Varanasi news sites, tourism pages and event
roundups. Pitch an actual story — the venue's history, a seasonal piece on how many
weddings the city hosts in a muhurat week — not a request for a link.

**7. Suppliers and associations.** Hotel and banquet associations, the local trader
body, any tourism or hospitality membership the business already holds. Members' pages
carry links.

## What to track

Once a quarter, check in Semrush (the account is already in use for the keyword work):
referring domains, new and lost links, and whether anything spammy has appeared
pointing at the site. Two numbers are enough — referring domains, and Google Business
Profile review count. Everything else is noise at this size.

## Honest expectation

A venue in a city this size does not need many links. Twenty to thirty genuine
referring domains, a complete Business Profile and a steady flow of reviews would put
City Garden ahead of most of its local competition, because the keyword difficulty in
this market is 18–23. The blog's job is to give those links somewhere worth pointing.
